Who is Ameenah?

Ameenah Ramsey is a Winnipeg-based photographer at the beginning of her creative journey. She is exploring different styles and perspectives while developing her skills and shaping her own approach to photography. Her work is driven by curiosity and a love for finding beauty in everyday moments, with each project offering a chance to learn, experiment, and grow.
